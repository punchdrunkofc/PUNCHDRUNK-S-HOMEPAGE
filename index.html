// ABOUT

THE STORY

PUNCHDRUNK.WAV is a digital hardcore / gabber project born somewhere between the warehouse rave scene and the distortion pedal. Started in 20XX by WHO, it exists at the intersection of hard dance, breakcore and cartoon violence.

The sound is built on broken kicks, acid lines and borderline-obnoxious distortion — a love letter to the era when hardcore was genuinely dangerous. Every track runs through the same two protagonists: Kenny and Maurice, a pair of cartoon mutants who exist to scream, fight and stomp. They are not mascots. They are the project's conscience.

All artwork, characters and visual identity are crafted in-house, with covers drawn by ARTIST_NAME. Find their work on Instagram.
