// ABOUT

THE STORY

PUNCHDRUNK.WAV is a digital hardcore / gabber project born on August 2, 2023. Blending digital hardcore, gabber, breakcore and breakbeat, it exists at the intersection of warehouse rave aggression and cartoon violence.

All artwork, characters and visual identity are crafted in-house, with covers drawn by FUBAR (previously by the artist Licorice). The project also releases free sample packs under the name PunchDrunk OFFICIAL — noise textures, distorted kicks and glitch elements for producers in the hardcore scene.
